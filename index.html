Established in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, NIBA Ventures is a leading construction and real estate company with over 15 years of on-ground experience. We have delivered residential townships, commercial complexes, and landmark institutional buildings across Central India.

From high-rise apartments and gated communities to large-scale institutional projects, we bring engineering rigour and proven construction expertise to every site. Our commitment is simple — deliver on time, within budget, and built to last.
