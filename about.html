Crafting Gwalior's Landmarks
for 15+ Years

Established in Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh, NIBA Ventures began with a vision to redefine construction and real estate in Central India. Founded by Ajay Bansal, the company has grown into one of the region's most trusted development groups with key ventures spanning residential townships, commercial complexes, and landmark institutional buildings. With a legacy of over 15 years, NIBA Ventures has consistently delivered growth, innovation, and client satisfaction across residential, commercial, and industrial spaces. Our developments combine aesthetics, functionality, and long-term value.

Our Legacy

Founded with the vision to redefine real estate in Gwalior and Central India, NIBA Ventures has consistently delivered projects that combine timely execution, premium quality, and thoughtful design.
